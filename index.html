How to play

Goal

Crypto words fall from the top of the board. Type a word's first letter to lock onto it, then keep typing it before it reaches the bottom line. A word you finish in time is destroyed and scores points; a word that reaches the bottom costs a life.

Typing

Tap the board (or click it with a mouse) to bring up your keyboard, then just start typing — there is no text box to aim at. A wrong letter while locked onto a word breaks your combo but does not lose the word; keep trying the next correct letter.

Scoring

Each finished word scores roughly twice its number of letters, boosted by your current combo. Finishing words back to back raises the combo multiplier; a wrong letter or a word reaching the bottom resets it to zero.

Modes

Daily: the same 20 words in the same order for everyone, new at 00:00 UTC. Clear all 20 without running out of lives to mark the day cleared. Quick 60: a free-for-all for 60 seconds, words fall a little faster as the clock runs down. Survival: endless, words keep speeding up, game over at 0 lives.

Lives and ranks

You start with 3 lives (4 for supporters). Your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy are shown at the end of each round. Total words typed correctly earns a rank: Newbie, Hodler (150), Diamond Hands (500), Whale (1,500) and Satoshi (4,000).

Supporters

A tip of 0.01 XNO or more unlocks the extra accent colours and a fourth life. Tips are optional and the whole game is free.
